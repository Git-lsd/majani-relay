Picture-card test

Label 60 coffee leaf photos

This test measures how well a person does with only a picture card, so we can compare it with the AI.

  1. Label each photo as you would on a plot visit, with only the picture card to help.
  2. Pick the problem you see, or Healthy. It is fine to say Not sure.
  3. Please do not look anything up or ask the others. There is no time limit; the page records how long each photo takes.
  4. Tap a photo or a card picture to see it bigger. Back lets you fix your last answer.

Your answers are kept on this device as you go, so a reload does not lose them. At the end, your name, your answers and the seconds per photo are sent to the laptop running the test. You will not see a score here.

Photos to label: RoCoLe dataset (Parraga-Alava, Cusme, Loor, Santander 2019), CC BY 4.0, resized.
